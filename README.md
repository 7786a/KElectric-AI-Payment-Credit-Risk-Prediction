## 📌 Project Overview

K-Electric, Pakistan's largest power utility serving **2.5M+ customers** in Karachi, faces two major financial challenges:

| Problem | Impact |
|---------|--------|
| 💰 **Payment Delays** | Customers delay bill payments, causing severe cash flow disruption |
| ⚠️ **Credit Risk** | High-risk customers cannot be identified before default, leading to bad debt |

**Current System:** Reactive - no mechanism to flag at-risk customers before default occurs

**Our Solution:** AI/ML-based dual prediction system for proactive financial management
